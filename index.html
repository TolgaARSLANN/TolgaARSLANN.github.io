Pawfect Care

Pet care platform · capstone project, extended in 2026

Feb 2025 – present

  • Full-stack app for tracking pets' health records, vaccines (with overdue and upcoming booster alerts), weight trends, medications and vet appointments, plus a community blog. The React frontend has lazy-loaded routes, light/dark themes and accessible forms.

Air quality early warning system
